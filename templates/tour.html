Local knowledge to make the most of your visit.

directions_subway

Getting There

local_taxi

Use Grab or Gojek rather than unmetered taxis — set the price before you get in if taking a regular cab.

directions_walk

Start the tour in the morning or late afternoon — midday heat and crowds make the experience harder.

map

Download the area map offline in Google Maps before you leave — data can be spotty in older districts.

volunteer_activism

Etiquette

checkroom

Dress modestly near religious sites — cover shoulders and knees. Sarongs are often available to borrow at temple entrances.

volume_off

Keep voices low near mosques, temples, and cemeteries — especially during active prayer or ceremony.

remove_shopping_cart

Bargain respectfully at markets — starting at half the asking price is usually fair. A firm no-thanks ends a negotiation cleanly.

restaurant

Food & Drink

storefront

Follow locals to warungs and hawker stalls near the stops — the best food is rarely near the tourist entrance.

water_drop

Carry a reusable water bottle — humidity in SEA makes this walk thirstier than it looks on a map.

photo_camera

Photography

wb_twilight

Golden hour (6–8am) gives colonial architecture and street scenes a warm glow with virtually no crowds.

person

Always ask permission before photographing people — a smile and a gesture go a long way even without a shared language.

no_photography

Photography is restricted inside most prayer halls and some museum rooms — look for signs or ask staff before shooting.
